# ecommers_sales_analysis_

Problem Statement:
In the modern retail landscape, e-commerce companies generate massive datasets from transactions across diverse global markets. 

The executive team is struggling to gain a unified view of business performance. Key challenges include:

1.  Profitability Leaks:     Identifying where high shipping costs are significantly impacting the net profit.
2.  Market Imbalance:        Determining which global markets (APAC, EU, US, LATAM) require more marketing investment versus those that are already self-sustaining.
3.  Inventory Focus:         Lack of clarity on which product categories drive the highest sales volume to optimize stock levels.
4.  Logistical Efficiency:   Understanding if the current "Ship Mode" distribution is aligned with sales targets.

## Project overview
This project features an interactive Data Analytics Dashboard designed to provide deep insights into global e-commerce sales performance. 
By transforming raw transactional data into visual intelligence, this tool helps businesses identify growth drivers, monitor profitability, and optimize logistics.

#Dashboard Components & KPIs
Sales Overview:           Tracks total revenue (currently at **$685.9K**).
Profitability Analysis:   Monitors net profit (**$91.28K**) and identifies high-margin segments.
Logistics Monitoring:     Visualizes shipping costs (**$112.93K**) to help identify potential cost-saving opportunities.
Regional Performance:     Country Level: Top 7 countries analyzed, with the **United States** leading at 37% of total sales.
    State Level:      High-granularity view showing **California** ($33.79K) and **New York** ($28.99K) as primary revenue hubs.
 Categorical Insights: Technology stands out as the dominant category with approximately **$0.69M** in sales.

## Tools and Technologies
* Data Visualization: Power BI 
* Data Processing Power Query (ETL)
* Analytics:  DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures.
*  Data Source:  E-commerce sales dataset (Excel/CSV).

   Business Insights
1. Market Leadership:The  APAC and **EU** regions represent the largest market shares, contributing over 48% of total sales combined.
2.Top Category: Technology  is the primary driver of sales volume; focusing marketing efforts here could yield the highest ROI.
3.   Shipping Impact:  Shipping costs are substantial; a shift toward more cost-effective shipping modes or regional warehousing could improve margins.
4.  Growth Opportunity:  High sales in **California** suggest a strong customer base that could be targeted for loyalty programs or localized promotions.
